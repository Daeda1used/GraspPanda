    Grouper¶

    Seed-based · 5 methods--set grouper=28 options

    The grouper looks at the neighbourhood of every seed in the frame of its approach direction and turns it into one feature. The head predicts angle, depth, width and score from that feature alone, so the grouper decides what the head can know about the local shape.

    Use it¶

    grasppanda compose graspnet_baseline/graspnet-baseline_pointnet2_g1b-realsense_18e \
    --set grouper=roi-grid -o graspnet-baseline_roi-grid.yaml
grasppanda compose graspnet_baseline/graspnet-baseline_pointnet2_g1b-realsense_18e --list grouper

    grasppanda compose gsnet/gsnet_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e \
    --set grouper=roi-grid -o gsnet_roi-grid.yaml
grasppanda compose gsnet/gsnet_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e --list grouper

    grasppanda compose scale_balanced_grasp/sbg_pointnet2-transformer_g1b-realsense_18e \
    --set grouper=roi-grid -o scale-balanced-grasp_roi-grid.yaml
grasppanda compose scale_balanced_grasp/sbg_pointnet2-transformer_g1b-realsense_18e --list grouper

    grasppanda compose economicgrasp/economicgrasp_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e \
    --set grouper=roi-grid -o economicgrasp_roi-grid.yaml
grasppanda compose economicgrasp/economicgrasp_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e --list grouper

    grasppanda compose finegrasp/finegrasp_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e \
    --set grouper=roi-grid -o finegrasp_roi-grid.yaml
grasppanda compose finegrasp/finegrasp_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e --list grouper

    The first command writes a checked config; the second lists every option of the slot under that method, with what was measured. Then grasppanda probe <file> tries the config on real frames and grasppanda train <file> trains it (Swap a component). Each method page lists the same: GraspNet-baseline · GSNet · Scale-Balanced-Grasp · EconomicGrasp · FineGrasp.

    What the columns say, and who is left out
    • Runs: out of the methods the option is a swap under, those under which its probe trained on real frames without error. A method that ships the option is named own and not counted; a method still undecided is counted and named. Measured on 20 training iterations on 8 RealSense frames of GraspNet-1Billion (one scene), seed 0, one run per cell; nothing was measured on GraspClutter6D or on Kinect frames.
    • Trains like shipped: out of the methods under which the swap ran, those under which every comparable loss term ended within the method's tolerance of the component it ships, with nothing rising; a method left out is named with why. Measured on 250 iterations on 8 RealSense frames of GraspNet-1Billion (one scene), seed 0, at a constant learning rate (FineGrasp: its own warm-up), beside the method as shipped trained the same way twice.
    • How we measure defines every word of these columns.

    Who is left out, and why:

    • depth-pool, runs: not GraspNet-baseline, Scale-Balanced-Grasp (incompatible by design).
    • multi-scale, trains like shipped: worse under GraspNet-baseline; diverging under Scale-Balanced-Grasp.
    • voxel-set-abstraction, runs: not GraspNet-baseline, Scale-Balanced-Grasp (not supported yet).

    Choosing¶

    • Match the head. A grouper returns one feature per seed or one per depth bin (the two tabs above), and the head must read the same; compose inserts depth-embed or depth-pool where they differ, and says so.
    • The shape of the crop. A cylinder along the approach axis is what the ported methods use; ball has no axis, box is a cylinder with a square section, knn follows the seed density.
    • Keep where a feature was found. roi-grid and roi-aware pool the cells of a gripper-aligned box instead of taking one maximum over the crop.
    • Several radii: multi-scale-linear fuses four crops with one linear layer, multi-scale with attention.
    • Sizes belong to the seed density. A crop among the seeds sees the seeds of the proposal; after changing one, check the other.

    Details¶

    The five steps of a crop

    Every published crop is the same five steps, and almost every option replaces one of them.

    Step What the ported methods do What the options add
    query: which points, in which shape a cylinder along the approach axis ball, k nearest, oriented box, grid of balls, grid of cells
    geometry of a neighbour offset in the crop frame the same with its length
    encoder per neighbour shared MLP (plus self-attention in EconomicGrasp) DGCNN edge feature, PointNeXt inverted residual, layers of PyTorch Geometric
    pooling maximum mean, attentive pooling, vector attention, all with a padding mask
    fusion, residual attention over radii (FineGrasp), linear + gate (Scale-Balanced-Grasp) linear, max or mean over radii; a projected seed residual
    Input and output 
    class Grouper(nn.Module):
    support: ClassVar[Literal["seeds", "scene"]]   # which points are grouped
    out_axes: ClassVar[tuple[str, ...]]            # ("seed", "channel") or ("seed", "depth", "channel")
    out_channels: int                              # width of the returned feature
    uses_seed_features: bool = False               # forward takes the seed features too

    def forward(self, support, centres, rotation[, seed_feats]) -> GroupFeatures: ...
    support points (N, 3) in metres, camera frame, with features (N, C). With support = "seeds" the seeds of the batch; with support = "scene" the whole scene cloud, with zero feature channels under a sub-sampling encoder.
    centres (M, 3) crop centre per seed: the seed at inference, the centre the assigner chose in training (so a crop may be empty).
    rotation (M, 3, 3) crop frame; column 0 is the approach axis. In training it may be a reflection: do not assume a right-handed frame.
    returns GroupFeatures: (M, out_channels) or (M, D, out_channels), one row per seed.

    What grasppanda compose checks before anything is built:

    • out_axes equals the head's in_axes. An axis is never added or squeezed implicitly; depth-embed and depth-pool do it explicitly.
    • One depth entry per depth bin of the head: the length of hmax, or num_depth, equals head.num_depth.
    • The width of the seed features a grouper adds to its crops equals what the encoder returns (below).

    The queries return the first nsample hits in index order and repeat the first hit in empty slots; a maximum does not notice the copies, a mean or an attention would, so the groupers mask them.

    The settings that matter

    LocalGroup: shape, encoder and pooling are options (all 48 combinations are legal and unit-tested).

    model:
  grouper:
    type: LocalGroup
    shape: cylinder          # ball | knn | box | cylinder
    radius: 0.05             # ball / cylinder radius, half side of the box (m)
    hmin: -0.02              # box and cylinder: ends along the approach axis (m)
    hmax: 0.04
    nsample: 16              # neighbours per crop (k of knn)
    encoder: mlp             # mlp | edge | invres
    pool: max                # max | mean | attentive | vector_attention
    mlp_channels: [256, 256] # the last width goes to the head

    edge tells the MLP how a neighbour differs from the seed; invres adds capacity after the pooling. max is robust to padding; vector_attention is the only pooling that reads the seed's own feature.

    DepthLocalGroup is the same over the scene, gathered once to the farthest reach; bin d pools the members with hmin < x < hmax[d], so near bins hold fewer members. seed_residual: projected works with any encoder width.

    RoI poolings (roi-grid, roi-aware) keep where in the box a feature was found. roi-aware leaves empty cells at zero, so with 1024 seeds most of its 64 cells are empty: try grid_size: 2 or 3. voxel-set-abstraction reads the scene and returns one feature per seed; scene_features: true needs a dense encoder.

    PyGConvGroup hands the crop to a layer of PyTorch Geometric as a bipartite graph: the members at their offsets in the crop frame, the seed at the origin, nothing padded. conv_args passes further arguments, for example --opts 'model.grouper.conv_args={heads: 8}'. Another layer needs three lines in PYG_CONVS (grasppanda/models/groupers/pyg.py).

    Several radii: MultiRadiusGroup runs one copy of branch per radius and fuses them (linear, max, mean); branch is any seeds-support grouper with a radius. MultiScaleGroup is the same with FineGrasp's attention over the scales.

    The depth adapters: depth-embed computes a per-seed crop once and tells the depth bins apart by a learned embedding; depth-pool takes the maximum over the bins of a scene crop. They make a swap possible; no paper proposes them.

    No grouper has released weights. To start the other stages from a checkpoint of the method, use runtime.load_from=<file> with runtime.allow_weight_mismatch=true.

    The width of the seed features

    Scale-Balanced-Grasp's crop adds sigmoid(W f) · f of the seed feature f to its 256-channel crop, so the encoder must return 256 channels. DepthCylinderGroup states the width it needs, and grasppanda compose compares it with the width the encoder declares. When they differ, compose puts DepthCylinderProjectionGroup in its place (the same crop with a linear layer in front of the residual) and says so; --no-adapter leaves the pair refused with the ways out. The composed model is then a variant of Scale-Balanced-Grasp, not the paper's.

    The projection is a class of its own on purpose: the config hash of a shipped config covers the constructor defaults of its components, so a new option on a ported class would change the hash of every config that uses it. MultiRadiusGroup beside MultiScaleGroup follows the same rule.

    Code and licences

    The LocalGroup and DepthLocalGroup parts are written here on grasppanda.ops from the papers (a few lines each; the official RandLA-Net code is CC BY-NC-SA and was not used). RoIGridGroup, RoIAwareGroup and VoxelSetAbstractionGroup adapt the pooling rules of OpenPCDet@233f849 (Apache-2.0) onto grasppanda.ops. PyGConvGroup is a bridge to torch_geometric (MIT; extra torch_geometric). CylinderGroup, CylinderInteractionGroup, MultiScaleGroup and DepthCylinderGroup are the crops of the ported methods. No grouper has weights.

    Pitfalls met while building this
    • Padding and non-max pooling. Empty slots repeat the first hit; with a mean or an attention those copies would outvote the real members. Use the mask crop_members returns in a pooling of your own.
    • Empty crops in training. The crop centre need not be a seed, so a seed-support crop may be empty. Its first slot then counts as the only member, so no pooling reduces an empty set.
    • Do not add an option to a component that shipped configs use. It changes their config hash, and every measured cell built from them stops matching what compose writes. New behaviour goes into a new class or a subclass.
    • A bridge passes the config check without its library. PyGConvGroup without torch_geometric stops when the model is built, with the install line.
    • knn over the scene is short-sighted. With 20 000 points the 64 nearest lie within about a centimetre, so the far depth bins of depth-knn see the same members as the near ones. Raise nsample or prefer a radius.
    • Memory. A scene-support crop holds seeds × nsample × width activations per layer (and per depth bin).
    Not wrapped
    Candidate Why not
    DOGraspNet's offset crop (code) a non-commercial licence; the idea is encoder: edge, written here.
    CUDA extensions of OpenPCDet and MMDetection3D compiled, not installed here, and they rotate about one axis only; the rules were adapted onto grasppanda.ops.
    Voxel RoI pooling (Voxel R-CNN), the multi-stride sources of PV-RCNN they read intermediate levels of a sparse encoder; the grouper gets the final features only.
    Closing-region crops (GPD, PointNetGPD, REGNet) they need the complete grasp pose, but the grouper runs before the head knows it; they belong to a re-scoring stage the skeleton does not have.
    Region patches of the image-to-region family (RegionNormalizedGrasp) no licence file, and the seeds of this slot are points, not image patches.
    KPConv, PosPool, point-pair features, Set-Transformer pooling not reached yet; each would be one more encoder or pool value of LocalGroup.
