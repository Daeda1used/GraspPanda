    Training recipe¶

    All 6 methods--set optimizer=--set schedule=--set hooks=--set augment=--set recipe=37 options

    The training recipe is everything around the model: how weights are updated, how the learning rate moves, what runs around the training loop and how a training sample is perturbed. None of it changes what the detector computes.

    Use it¶

    grasppanda compose graspnet_baseline/graspnet-baseline_pointnet2_g1b-realsense_18e \
    --set optimizer=adamw -o graspnet-baseline_adamw.yaml
grasppanda compose graspnet_baseline/graspnet-baseline_pointnet2_g1b-realsense_18e --list optimizer

    grasppanda compose gsnet/gsnet_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e \
    --set optimizer=adamw -o gsnet_adamw.yaml
grasppanda compose gsnet/gsnet_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e --list optimizer

    grasppanda compose scale_balanced_grasp/sbg_pointnet2-transformer_g1b-realsense_18e \
    --set optimizer=adamw -o scale-balanced-grasp_adamw.yaml
grasppanda compose scale_balanced_grasp/sbg_pointnet2-transformer_g1b-realsense_18e --list optimizer

    grasppanda compose economicgrasp/economicgrasp_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e \
    --set optimizer=adamw -o economicgrasp_adamw.yaml
grasppanda compose economicgrasp/economicgrasp_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e --list optimizer

    grasppanda compose finegrasp/finegrasp_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e \
    --set optimizer=adamw -o finegrasp_adamw.yaml
grasppanda compose finegrasp/finegrasp_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e --list optimizer

    grasppanda compose hggd/hggd_r34w8_g1b-realsense_15e \
    --set optimizer=adamw -o hggd_adamw.yaml
grasppanda compose hggd/hggd_r34w8_g1b-realsense_15e --list optimizer

    The first command writes a checked config; the second lists every option of the slot under that method, with what was measured. Then grasppanda probe <file> tries the config on real frames and grasppanda train <file> trains it (Swap a component). Each method page lists the same: GraspNet-baseline · GSNet · Scale-Balanced-Grasp · EconomicGrasp · FineGrasp · HGGD.

    optimizer, schedule and recipe take one preset; hooks and augment take several joined by +, as in --set hooks=ema+grad-clip. --opts changes single values and wins over a preset: --opts optimizer.lr=3e-3 train.epochs=20.

    What the columns say, and who is left out
    • Runs: out of the methods the option is a swap under, those under which its probe trained on real frames without error. A method that ships the option is named own and not counted; a method still undecided is counted and named. Measured on 20 training iterations on 8 RealSense frames of GraspNet-1Billion (one scene), seed 0, one run per cell; nothing was measured on GraspClutter6D or on Kinect frames.
    • Trains like shipped: out of the methods under which the swap ran, those under which every comparable loss term ended within the method's tolerance of the component it ships, with nothing rising; a method left out is named with why. Measured on 250 iterations on 8 RealSense frames of GraspNet-1Billion (one scene), seed 0, at a constant learning rate (FineGrasp: its own warm-up), beside the method as shipped trained the same way twice.
    • Schedules have no level 2: level 2 holds the schedule of the method as shipped fixed (a constant rate; FineGrasp: its own warm-up), so a schedule swap is not compared.
    • How we measure defines every word of these columns.

    Choosing¶

    • The method's own recipe stays where no preset replaces a part; the composed file lists what each preset says must be re-tuned.
    • A pretrained scene encoder: --set recipe=finetune-encoder, or its parts optimizer=encoder-lr-0.1, optimizer=layer-decay and hooks=freeze-encoder. They name the scene encoder, so they apply to the seed-based methods only.
    • Learning-rate-free and schedule-free optimizers: prodigy wants lr: 1.0; each schedulefree-* preset sets the optimizer, no schedule and its hook together.
    • One weight average per run: ema or swa; evaluation.weights: ema scores the average.
    • Point augmentation moves points, which a method that reads images cannot follow; dropout removes points and moves none.

    Details¶

    Where the recipe lives in a config
    Config key Registry What it decides When it runs
    optimizer OPTIMIZERS how gradients become weight updates once per train.accumulate batches
    scheduler SCHEDULERS the learning rate over the run after every optimizer step or every epoch (train.scheduler_unit)
    hooks HOOKS anything else around the loop: weight averages, freezing, clipping variants at before_run, before_epoch, before_step, after_iter, after_epoch, after_run
    data.train.dataset.transforms TRANSFORMS augmentation of one training sample in the data-loader workers, per sample

    Plain options of the loop need no component: train.amp, train.accumulate, train.grad_clip with train.grad_clip_mode, train.ema, train.sync_bn. Three method-neutral files under configs/_base_/schedules/ can be inherited without compose: adamw_onecycle_10e.yaml, adamw_warmup-cosine_10e.yaml, schedulefree-adamw_10e.yaml.

    Input and output
    • Optimizer. Built from the optimizer node; the engine supplies params (or what the detector's configure_optimizers returns) and, where asked, model. The bridges return the library's own optimizer, so checkpoints hold its state unchanged.
    • Scheduler. Built per optimizer; the trainer steps it per optimizer step (train.scheduler_unit: iteration) or per epoch. The engine fills in the length of the run where a schedule needs it. scheduler: null is a constant rate.
    • Hook. A subclass of grasppanda.engine.hooks.Hook; every callback receives the training state. Hooks run in ascending priority (evaluation is 70, checkpointing 80). A weight average installs itself as state.ema, which evaluation.weights: ema scores.
    • Transform. Maps one PointSample to one PointSample. It may remove or reorder points only through sample.gather(index), which carries the labels; move the scene only with the object poses; and not move points at all when the sample carries images, as HGGD's does (the config check refuses that list). Scaling the scene is not allowed: grasp widths, depths and the gripper are in metres.
    • The config check tests every key against the constructor of the named class without importing the optional libraries; free-form arguments of a library go into options. A wrong optimizer name is found when the optimizer is built, with the list of valid ones.
    Optimizers and parameter groups 
    optimizer: {type: AdamW, lr: 1.0e-3, weight_decay: 0.05}                    # torch.optim, by class
optimizer: {type: TimmOptimizer, name: lamb, lr: 2.0e-3, weight_decay: 0.02}
optimizer: {type: PytorchOptimizer, name: prodigy, lr: 1.0}
optimizer: {type: ScheduleFree, name: adamw, lr: 2.5e-3, warmup_steps: 200}
    • lr and weight_decay are arguments of the bridge; everything else (betas, eps, momentum) goes into options. A lookahead_ prefix wraps any timm optimizer.
    • A schedule-free optimizer needs scheduler: null and ScheduleFreeHook; its presets set all three.

    ParamGroups wraps any optimizer with a rate per module and layer-wise decay:

    optimizer:
  type: ParamGroups
  optimizer: {type: AdamW, lr: 1.0e-3, weight_decay: 0.05}
  lr_multipliers: {backbone: 0.1}      # dotted prefix of the parameter name, or "re:<regex>"
  layer_decay: 0.75                    # null: off
  layer_decay_module: backbone
  layer_decay_depth: 2
  no_decay_1d: true                    # no weight decay on biases and normalisation weights

    The rate of a parameter is lr × the first matching multiplier × layer_decay ** (L - i) for layer i of the L layers of layer_decay_module, counted in registration order (a U-Net's decoder counts as later layers). To train a pretrained encoder, load its weights with runtime.load_from=<checkpoint> and runtime.allow_weight_mismatch=true, then use the encoder presets.

    Schedules, hooks and augmentation 
    scheduler: {type: OneCycleLR, max_lr: 1.0e-3, pct_start: 0.05, div_factor: 10.0, cycle_momentum: false}
scheduler: {type: TimmSchedule, name: cosine, warmup_steps: 0.05, warmup_lr_init: 1.0e-6, lr_min: 1.0e-6}
hooks:
  - {type: EMAHook, decay: 0.999, warmup: true, interval: 1, buffers: copy}
  - {type: FreezeHook, modules: [backbone], epochs: 2, eval_mode: true}
evaluation: {weights: ema}
    • TimmSchedule.warmup_steps below 1 is a fraction of the run, from 1 a step count; warmup_lr_init and lr_min are absolute rates.
    • EMAHook, SWAHook and train.ema exclude each other. EMAHook warms its decay up and copies buffers by default (batch-norm statistics and HGGD's learned anchors stay those of the trained model).
    • FreezeHook sets requires_grad per epoch, so nothing is rebuilt when modules are released. AdaptiveGradClipHook and train.grad_clip exclude each other.
    • --set augment=dropout+shift+jitter writes RandomDropout before the sampling step and RandomShift and RandomJitter after the base list. Units are metres in the camera frame; RandomJitter perturbs only the network input.
    Components and their sources

    pip install -e ".[optim]" installs timm, pytorch_optimizer and schedulefree (all Apache-2.0); the rest needs nothing beyond the core install.

    TimmOptimizer, TimmSchedule optimizer, schedule any optimizer or schedule of timm by name bridge to timm.optim, timm.scheduler
    PytorchOptimizer optimizer any optimizer of pytorch_optimizer by name bridge to pytorch_optimizer.load_optimizer

    EMAHook, SWAHook hook weight averages (arXiv 1703.01780, arXiv 1803.05407) torch.optim.swa_utils.AveragedModel

    AdaptiveGradClipHook hook adaptive gradient clipping (arXiv 2102.06171) bridge to timm.utils.adaptive_clip_grad
    RandomJitter, RandomDropout, RandomShift transform noise on the points, removal of points with their labels, a shift of the scene and the object poses adapted from Pointcept @ 1342eda (MIT)
    Pitfalls met while building this
    • OneCycleLR and momentum. Its default cycle_momentum: true rewrites betas or momentum every step; the presets set false. Its peak is max_lr: changing optimizer.lr changes nothing.
    • Schedule-free and batch norm. The evaluated weights are an average, but the batch-norm statistics were collected at the training point; this toolbox does not re-estimate them.
    • Short probes and warm-ups. With warmup_steps: 200 a 20-iteration probe runs inside the warm-up, so its losses fall slowly; that says nothing about a full run.
    • Per-epoch units and warm-up. With train.scheduler_unit: epoch a warm-up from 0 spends the first epoch at rate 0; multistep-warmup starts at 1e-4.
    • Lists replace. hooks and transforms of a child config replace the inherited list; compose writes the base's entries out again, a hand-written child must repeat them (HGGD needs its EpochSignalHook).
    • SWAHook.start_epoch must be below train.epochs; the run refuses to start otherwise. A short probe ends before averaging starts, so such a probe says nothing about fit.
    • Mixed precision is refused for the ported methods. When it was tried, the label assigner of the seed-based skeleton wrote half-precision values into a single-precision tensor, and HGGD's first loss was not finite. hooks=amp sets train.amp: true, which the config check now refuses unless the detector declares amp_ready = True; none of the shipped detectors does. The preset is the switch for a detector of your own.
    • A wrong optimizer name passes the config check and fails when the optimizer is built, with the list of valid names.
    Not wrapped
    Candidate Why not
    Normalisation and activation as config keys no shared layer builder exists in the stages; a hook that swapped modules after construction would not run at test time.
    RandomScale, ElasticDistortion grasp widths, depths and the gripper are in metres, and labels attach to rigid object poses.
    Chromatic transforms the scene encoders read no colour; HGGD's image pipeline is not a point transform.
    Mix3D scene mixing works in the collate function, and two scenes' labels would have to be merged.
    Sharpness-aware minimisation (SAM) its step() needs a second forward and backward pass.
    ReduceLROnPlateau steps on a monitored metric, which the trainer does not hand to schedules; refused by name.
    Batch-norm re-estimation after SWA update_bn iterates plain tensors; batches here are Batch objects.
