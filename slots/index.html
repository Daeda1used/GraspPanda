    Components¶

    GraspPanda builds a grasp detector from stages, and every stage is a slot: a place in the config where one component can be swapped for another with one line, --set <slot>=<option>. Five methods share the seed-based skeleton and its slots; HGGD reads images and has one slot of its own, the image encoder. Post-processing and the training recipe apply to all six methods. In all, 244 options you can --set.

    The slots¶

    • Image encoder

      Turns the colour image into feature maps; HGGD predicts its grasp heat-map from them.

      HGGD only 38 options

      --set image_encoder=

    • Scene encoder

      One feature vector per point of the depth cloud: what a seed knows before any crop. By family: point-based · sparse-voxel · transformers.

      Seed-based · 5 methods 47 options

      --set backbone=

    • Seed proposal

      Picks the points of the scene at which grasps are predicted.

      Seed-based · 5 methods 24 options

      --set proposal=

    • View

      Chooses the approach direction of every seed.

      Seed-based · 5 methods 21 options

      --set view=

    • Grouper

      Gathers the points around a seed into the feature the head reads.

      Seed-based · 5 methods 28 options

      --set grouper=

    • Heads and assigners

      The head predicts the grasp at a seed; the assigner matches seeds with labels in training.

      Seed-based · 5 methods 10 head bundles · 4 assigners

      --set head= --set assigner=

    • Losses

      One term of the loss of a stage, replaced on its own.

      Seed-based · 5 methods 20 options

      --set <stage>.<term>_loss=

    • Post-processing

      Filters, selects and corrects predicted grasps before a robot or an evaluator.

      All 6 methods 15 options

      --set post=

    • Training recipe

      How weights are updated, how the rate moves, what runs around the loop, how a sample is perturbed.

      All 6 methods 17 optimizers · 7 schedules · 7 hooks · 3 augmentations · 3 whole recipes

      --set optimizer= --set schedule= …

    Words used on these pages¶

    Seed, preset, contract, adapter, label set
    • Seed: a point of the scene at which a grasp is predicted; the seed proposal picks them (1024 per frame in each of the five seed-based methods).
    • Preset: a small YAML file with a component and settings for table-top depth clouds; its name is what follows --set <slot>=.
    • Contract: what a component declares about its input and output. grasppanda compose compares contracts before anything is built and refuses a mismatch with the fix.
    • Adapter: a wrapper compose puts around a scene encoder so that it fits the next stage.
    • Label set: the grasp labels a head is trained on, dense (every view, angle and depth of a labelled point) or compact (the best grasp per view). A head, its assigner and the dataset section use the same one.
