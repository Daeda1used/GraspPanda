    Heads and assigners¶

    Seed-based · 5 methods--set head=--set assigner=14 options

    The head bundle predicts the grasp at every seed: in-plane rotation, depth, width and score. The assigner matches every seed with the grasp labels it is trained on.

    Use it¶

    grasppanda compose graspnet_baseline/graspnet-baseline_pointnet2_g1b-realsense_18e \
    --set head=swad-focal -o graspnet-baseline_swad-focal.yaml
grasppanda compose graspnet_baseline/graspnet-baseline_pointnet2_g1b-realsense_18e --list head

    grasppanda compose gsnet/gsnet_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e \
    --set head=swad-focal -o gsnet_swad-focal.yaml
grasppanda compose gsnet/gsnet_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e --list head

    grasppanda compose scale_balanced_grasp/sbg_pointnet2-transformer_g1b-realsense_18e \
    --set head=swad-focal -o scale-balanced-grasp_swad-focal.yaml
grasppanda compose scale_balanced_grasp/sbg_pointnet2-transformer_g1b-realsense_18e --list head

    grasppanda compose economicgrasp/economicgrasp_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e \
    --set head=swad-focal -o economicgrasp_swad-focal.yaml
grasppanda compose economicgrasp/economicgrasp_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e --list head

    grasppanda compose finegrasp/finegrasp_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e \
    --set head=swad-focal -o finegrasp_swad-focal.yaml
grasppanda compose finegrasp/finegrasp_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e --list head

    The first command writes a checked config; the second lists every option of the slot under that method, with what was measured. Then grasppanda probe <file> tries the config on real frames and grasppanda train <file> trains it (Swap a component). Each method page lists the same: GraspNet-baseline · GSNet · Scale-Balanced-Grasp · EconomicGrasp · FineGrasp.

    An assigner is set the same way, --set assigner=<option>.

    Options¶

    A probe shows that a combination trains on real frames of GraspNet-1Billion, not how good it is. Accuracy is claimed only for a method trained and evaluated here.

    What the columns say, and who is left out
    • Runs: out of the methods the option is a swap under, those under which its probe trained on real frames without error. A method that ships the option is named own and not counted; a method still undecided is counted and named. Measured on 20 training iterations on 8 RealSense frames of GraspNet-1Billion (one scene), seed 0, one run per cell; nothing was measured on GraspClutter6D or on Kinect frames.
    • Trains like shipped: out of the methods under which the swap ran, those under which every comparable loss term ended within the method's tolerance of the component it ships, with nothing rising; a method left out is named with why. Measured on 250 iterations on 8 RealSense frames of GraspNet-1Billion (one scene), seed 0, at a constant learning rate (FineGrasp: its own warm-up), beside the method as shipped trained the same way twice.
    • How we measure defines every word of these columns.

    • economic-focal, runs: not GraspNet-baseline, Scale-Balanced-Grasp (incompatible by design).
    • nearest-label-object-seeds, runs: own in GraspNet-baseline; not EconomicGrasp, FineGrasp (incompatible by design).
    • NearestLabelAssigner, runs: own in GSNet; not EconomicGrasp, FineGrasp (incompatible by design).
    • NearestLabelAssigner, trains like shipped: not comparable under GraspNet-baseline; diverging under Scale-Balanced-Grasp.
    • scale-balanced, runs: own in Scale-Balanced-Grasp; not EconomicGrasp, FineGrasp (incompatible by design).

    Choosing¶

    • A head is swapped whole. Its network, target encoder, loss and decoder share one way of writing a grasp. Its loss terms are swapped one by one on Losses.
    • Head, assigner and labels go together. A head is trained on dense label grids or on compact best grasps; compose switches the assigner and the dataset section to the head's label set and says so.
    • The crop decides the head. A head reads one feature per seed or one per depth bin; Grouper lists which crop gives which.
    • Classify or regress depth and width: decoupled-grid-class and decoupled-grid-regression are the two ends; decoupled-grid-bins predicts the width as a distribution.
    • Scores of two heads are not comparable. A score threshold fitted to one head does not carry over to another.

    Details¶

    Input and output of a head bundle 
    class HeadBundle(nn.Module):
    def forward(self, crops: GroupFeatures) -> Predictions: ...        # raw outputs, with gradients
    def encode(self, matched: MatchedLabels, ...) -> Targets: ...      # labels -> targets
    def loss(self, predictions, targets, ...) -> dict[str, Tensor]:    # named, weighted terms
    def decode(self, raw, seeds, approach, gripper) -> GraspGroup: ... # one grasp per seed
    in: crop features from the grouper, one row per valid seed, with the grouper's out_axes: ('seed', 'channel') or ('seed', 'depth', 'channel').
    in: matched labels from the assigner, reduced to the chosen view: dense (a friction and a width per in-plane angle and depth bin) or compact (one best grasp per seed).
    out, training named loss terms, each multiplied by its weight; the trainer sums them and logs each as loss/<name>.
    out, prediction a GraspGroup with one grasp per seed: translation (the seed), rotation (approach from the view stage, in-plane angle from the head), depth, width (metres), score.
    Attribute Meaning Rule
    in_axes axes of the crop features it expects must equal the grouper's out_axes
    label_format dense or compact must equal the assigner's (and the dataset must serve that label set)
    score_kind meaning of the decoded score scores of different kinds are not comparable
    applies_seed_weights whether its loss honours a weight per seed an assigner that returns weights needs a head that applies them
    loss_terms loss option → kind of term every loss node is checked against it (Losses)
    The assigner

    The assigner runs in training only. It matches every seed with the labels of the label point it sits on and hands them, reduced to the chosen view, to the view stage and the head. So it is chosen with the head and the label set, not on its own.

    • NearestLabelAssigner (its defaults, no preset): every seed takes the labels of the nearest label point of its object; every seed is supervised.
    • nearest-label-object-seeds: the same matching; only seeds on an object are supervised, and training crops sit at the matched label point.
    • compact-label: one labelled grasp per seed at its chosen view, only within 5 mm of a label point; it brings the dataset section compact_labels.
    • scale-balanced: the matching above plus a loss weight per seed, larger for rare grasp widths. It needs the width histogram of the training split (grasppanda prepare graspnet1b --scale-statistic; configs/scale_balanced_grasp/README.md); compose adds the dataset section scale_statistic and says so.

    grasppanda compose --show assigner=scale-balanced prints a preset with what must be re-tuned.

    The settings of DecoupledGridHead 
    model:
  head:
    type: DecoupledGridHead
    num_angle: 12
    num_depth: 4
    depth_step: 0.01
    depth: class                  # class | regression (a continuous depth between the bins)
    width: bins                   # regression | class (bin centre) | bins (expectation)
    width_bins: 16                # for class and bins: 16 over 0.1 m is a bin every 6.7 mm
    score_target: log_friction_fixed_range
    friction_range: [0.1, 1.0]    # the range of the GraspNet-1Billion labels
    width_loss: {type: DistributionFocal, weight: 1.0}
    • The kind of depth_loss and width_loss follows the options depth and width, so a loss must change with them (the presets do).
    • The class terms start near ln 12 and ln 4, a regressed width is a distance in metres × 10: watch loss/angle and loss/depth beside loss/score and re-tune the weights.
    • width_decode_gain: 1.2 opens the gripper 20 % wider than learned, as the other heads of the family do; 1.0 decodes what was learned.
    • A head and a loss combine: --set head=decoupled-grid --set head.score_loss=varifocal.

    DecoupledGridHead is written here after the idea of the grasp head of FGC-GraspNet (arXiv 2206.11141, ICRA 2022), without its code. No head has released weights of its own.

    Pitfalls met while building this

    • A label set is a contract. Compose switches the labels and the assigner for a head of the other label set, and the dense grids then come from the label store of grasppanda prepare <dataset> --labels. A config written by hand with a mismatch is refused:

      ✗ assigner · head: CompactLabelAssigner returns 'compact' matched labels, but
  DecoupledGridHead is supervised with 'dense' ones. A head bundle and the assigner
  that feeds it must agree on the label set; the dataset must serve that label set too.

    • A logit loss changes what the score is. A score threshold fitted on the unbounded score of swad does not carry over to swad-focal, whose score lies in [0, 1].

    • SWADHead with score_target: log_friction_batch_norm (the GSNet reference) normalises targets with the extrema of the batch; log_friction_fixed_range gives a target that means the same in every batch (swad-focal sets it).
    • The extra class of EconomicHead. With unmatched_class: true every classifier has one output that is never supervised; label smoothing gives it target mass. Use decode_argmax: supervised (as economic-focal does) or unmatched_class: false.
    • Seed weights. A stage that receives the weights of ScaleBalancedAssigner must apply them (applies_seed_weights); a loss that scores the supervised set as a whole cannot, so it is refused there (Losses).
