    View¶

    Seed-based · 5 methods--set view=21 options

    The view stage chooses the approach direction of every seed: two of the three degrees of freedom of the grasp orientation. The grouper crops the scene in the frame it chooses, so it decides what the head gets to see.

    Use it¶

    grasppanda compose graspnet_baseline/graspnet-baseline_pointnet2_g1b-realsense_18e \
    --set view=lattice-view -o graspnet-baseline_lattice-view.yaml
grasppanda compose graspnet_baseline/graspnet-baseline_pointnet2_g1b-realsense_18e --list view

    grasppanda compose gsnet/gsnet_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e \
    --set view=lattice-view -o gsnet_lattice-view.yaml
grasppanda compose gsnet/gsnet_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e --list view

    grasppanda compose scale_balanced_grasp/sbg_pointnet2-transformer_g1b-realsense_18e \
    --set view=lattice-view -o scale-balanced-grasp_lattice-view.yaml
grasppanda compose scale_balanced_grasp/sbg_pointnet2-transformer_g1b-realsense_18e --list view

    grasppanda compose economicgrasp/economicgrasp_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e \
    --set view=lattice-view -o economicgrasp_lattice-view.yaml
grasppanda compose economicgrasp/economicgrasp_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e --list view

    grasppanda compose finegrasp/finegrasp_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e \
    --set view=lattice-view -o finegrasp_lattice-view.yaml
grasppanda compose finegrasp/finegrasp_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e --list view

    The first command writes a checked config; the second lists every option of the slot under that method, with what was measured. Then grasppanda probe <file> tries the config on real frames and grasppanda train <file> trains it (Swap a component). Each method page lists the same: GraspNet-baseline · GSNet · Scale-Balanced-Grasp · EconomicGrasp · FineGrasp.

A method that ships the option is named own and not counted; a method still undecided is counted and named.     Compare under every method

    Choosing¶

    • The labels know 300 directions. Every option snaps its choice to one of the 300 label views for training, so a finer lattice does not give finer labels.
    • Fewer classes: lattice-fibonacci-120, icosphere-162 or healpix-192; only approaches away from the camera: lattice-hemisphere-60.
    • A classification instead of 300 regressions: lattice-best-view; lattice-angular-cost makes near misses cheap.
    • No lattice at all: direction-regression, with a confidence direction-vmf; a whole grasp frame: two-vector or rotation-6d.
    • The head keeps the in-plane angle. A stage that regresses a full rotation contributes its approach axis; the rest of the frame is supervised and logged, not decoded.

    Details¶

    Which option for which need
    You want Take Why
    the view scoring of GSNet, EconomicGrasp and FineGrasp template-view a score per label view; the training view is sampled from the scores
    the view stage of GraspNet-baseline or Scale-Balanced-Grasp objectness-view, objectness-view-graspable also predicts objectness, which their heads read
    the same scoring as a base for the options below lattice-view computes what template-view computes, on a lattice of any size
    a smaller output layer lattice-fibonacci-120, icosphere-162, healpix-192 fewer classes; each view is supervised by its nearest label view
    only approaches away from the camera lattice-hemisphere-60 the 60-of-120 template of RGB Matters
    a classification of the best view lattice-best-view, lattice-angular-cost cross-entropy on the most graspable view; the second adds the angular class loss of RGB Matters
    a direction between lattice points at inference lattice-soft-argmax softmax-weighted mean of the best view directions
    training crops closer to what inference chooses lattice-topk samples the training view among the 10 best views
    no lattice in the output direction-regression, direction-vmf 3 numbers per seed and a cosine loss; or a von Mises-Fisher likelihood with a concentration
    a full grasp frame two-vector, two-vector-baseline, rotation-6d, rotation-9d, rotation-quaternion Gram-Schmidt of two vectors, or the rotation mappings of RoMa
    Input and output

    approach, seeds = view(seeds), and view.loss(approach, matched) for its own loss.
    receives SeedSet: M seeds of the batch, coordinates (M, 3) in metres in the camera frame and features (M, C); C is injected (in_channels)
    returns ApproachSet, one row per seed, and the SeedSet (a stage may add a residual to the features, a score, or mark seeds invalid)
    direction (M, 3) unit vectors; read by the decoder of every head: rotation = approach_to_rotation(direction, in-plane angle)
    rotation (M, 3, 3), column 0 the direction; the crop frame of the grouper at inference
    view_index (M,) index into view.template, the 300 views the labels are indexed by; the assigner looks the labels up there in training
    declares label_formats (dense or compact labels it can read), needs_matched, seed_scores (scores it adds), applies_seed_weights

    Three facts shape every option:

    1. The labels know 300 directions. GraspNet-1Billion and GraspClutter6D labels are indexed by a 300-view Fibonacci lattice. A stage may predict on another lattice or none, but it snaps its choice to one of those views for the label lookup.
    2. Train and inference crops differ by the snap. In training the crop frame is the label frame of the snapped view; at inference it is the direction the stage returned.
    3. The head owns the in-plane angle. Every head decodes approach_to_rotation(direction, angle bin).

    What each kind of stage needs:

    Stage From the labels From the assigner From the head
    lattice stages (Lattice, Icosphere, Healpix) the graspness of all 300 label views per seed (dense or compact) the label lookup at view_index nothing
    DirectionViewStage the same, reduced to the most graspable view the same nothing
    TwoVectorViewStage, RomaRotationViewStage the same, plus the grid or angle bin of the snapped view also crop_rotation, the label frame of the snapped view the same num_angle (checked)

    No new stage has released weights. A checkpoint of a ported method loaded into a model with another view stage leaves only that stage untrained.

    The settings that matter

    A lattice stage:

    model:
  view:
    type: LatticeViewStage        # or IcosphereViewStage (subdivisions) / HealpixViewStage (nside)
    num_views: 300
    hemisphere: false             # true: only approaches away from the camera
    target: score                 # score | best_view
    selection: argmax             # inference: argmax | soft_argmax
    train_selection: multinomial  # training: multinomial | topk | argmax
    top_k: null                   # used by soft_argmax and topk
    temperature: 1.0              # softmax temperature of soft_argmax
    angular_weight: 0.0           # best_view only: weight of the angular class loss
    • Size. An icosphere has 10 · 4**subdivisions + 2 views, HEALPix 12 · nside**2. Beyond 300 views neighbouring views share one label column.
    • target: score regresses the normalised graspness of every view (GSNet's). target: best_view classifies the most graspable view; use it with train_selection: argmax and a CrossEntropy loss.
    • selection: soft_argmax changes inference only. A temperature of about 0.05 fits regressed scores in [0, 1], 1.0 fits logits; keep top_k small (8).

    A regression stage:

    model:
  view:
    type: TwoVectorViewStage
    keep: approach                # approach (6-D order) | baseline (Contact-GraspNet order)
    direction_weight: 1.0         # loss/view: 1 - cos to the most graspable label view
    frame_weight: 1.0             # loss/view_frame: distance to the label frame, half-turn aware
    num_angle: 12                 # must equal the head's
    • loss/view_frame takes the smaller of the distances to the label frame and to its half-turn copy: a parallel-jaw grasp turned by 180° about its approach is the same grasp.
    • representation of RomaRotationViewStage: 6d and 9d are continuous; quaternion and rotvec are there for comparison (rotvec has no preset and no probe record).
    • residual: true adds the hidden features of the stage to the seed features, as GSNet's stage does.

    Pitfalls met while building this

    • A finer lattice does not give finer labels. Angles for 200 000 random directions, computed with the lattices of grasppanda.ops:

      Lattice Views Random direction to nearest view, mean / max View to its nearest label view, mean / max Label columns read
      Fibonacci (the labels) 300 4.5° / 9.0° 0° 300
      Fibonacci 120 7.1° / 14.1° 4.6° / 7.7° 120
      icosphere 162 6.1° / 10.8° 4.5° / 7.7° 162
      icosphere 642 3.0° / 5.4° 4.5° / 8.1° 300
      HEALPix 192 5.6° / 11.5° 4.5° / 7.9° 190
      HEALPix 768 2.8° / 5.6° 4.5° / 8.4° 300

      The third column is the price of a coarse lattice; the fourth is the crop mismatch of any lattice that is not the label lattice.

    • TemplateViewStage(num_views=N) with N ≠ 300 is not another lattice size. Its template is also the label lattice, so the assigner refuses the labels. Use LatticeViewStage(num_views=N).

    • Soft-arg-max needs a temperature that fits the outputs. With regressed scores and a temperature of 1 the weights are nearly uniform; with every view voting, opposite views can cancel (the stage then falls back to the best view).
    • A single best view is a brittle target. On a symmetric object two distant views can tie and the target flips between them; target: score and angular_weight suffer less.
    • Flip augmentation yields mirrored label frames. The frame loss skips those seeds; their direction loss still counts.
    • The vMF loss is a log-density and goes negative when the concentration is high.
    • healpy is GPL-2.0, so the bridge uses the BSD-licensed healpix package.
    Not wrapped
    Candidate Why not
    Contact-GraspNet code (NVlabs/contact_graspnet) its licence limits use to non-commercial research. Its parameterisation is two-vector-baseline; its contact labels need an asset the toolbox does not have.
    vMF-Contact, Generalizing-Grasp and S4G code no licence files. The closed-form likelihood, the cosine regression and the 6-D representation are written here; their other terms are not.
    DexGraspNet 2.0 (diffusion on seed features) no licence file; a generative head is a head, not a view stage.
    OrbitGrasp, Edge Grasp Network detectors of their own with their own simulated labels.
    PyTorch3D rotation conversions a compiled package for functions RoMa has in pure PyTorch.
    Several grasps per seed at inference ApproachSet has one row per seed; it needs a change of the skeleton.
    A head that takes the regressed in-plane rotation belongs to the head slot; until it exists the frame of the rotation stages is auxiliary.
