    Slot view: approach direction and orientation¶

    Words such as seed, contract, preset, adapter, label set and point painting are explained in the slot index.

    Which of these options fit a method

    Every method has a page that lists the options of each of its slots with what was measured for each (the verdict, the levels composes, runs, learns and predicts, the class, the grasps, the reason of a refusal): GSNet (view); EconomicGrasp (view); FineGrasp (view); GraspNet-baseline (view); Scale-Balanced-Grasp (view). In a terminal: grasppanda compose gsnet/gsnet_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e --list view.

    Class: one run, seed 0, 20 iterations, 8 frames of one scene; a class counts verdicts, not accuracy. Where this page names a class or a measured verdict, it is one of the measured classes.

    Nothing on this page carries an AP. "Runs" means: the combination passes the contract check, builds, trains 20 iterations on real GraspNet-1Billion frames with finite losses and predicts. How good a choice is takes a full training and an evaluation.

    1. What the slot does¶

    model.view is the third stage of the seed-based detectors (GSNet, EconomicGrasp, FineGrasp, GraspNet-baseline, Scale-Balanced-Grasp):

    backbone → proposal → VIEW → (assigner, training only) → grouper → head bundle

    A 6-DoF grasp orientation has three degrees of freedom. The view stage fixes the first two, the approach direction (the unit vector from the gripper towards the object). The head bundle then predicts the third one, the in-plane rotation about that direction, together with depth and width, from a crop of the scene that the grouper cuts in the frame the view stage chose. So the view stage decides what the head gets to look at.

    Input and output¶
    Called as approach, seeds = view(seeds)
    Receives SeedSet: M seeds of the whole batch (frames concatenated), seeds.points.coords (M, 3) in metres in the camera frame, seeds.feats (M, C); C is injected by the detector (in_channels)
    Returns ApproachSet with one row per seed, and the SeedSet (a stage may add a residual to feats, add a score to scores, or mark seeds invalid)
    ApproachSet.direction (M, 3) unit vectors, camera frame. Read by the decoder of every head bundle: the grasp rotation is approach_to_rotation(direction, in-plane angle)
    ApproachSet.rotation (M, 3, 3), column 0 = the direction, column 1 horizontal (approach_to_rotation). Read by the grouper at inference as the crop frame. Required
    ApproachSet.view_index (M,) index into view.template, the lattice the label files are indexed by (300 Fibonacci views). Read by the assigner in training, which looks up the labels of that view and returns the training crop frame (object pose × template frame of that view). Required by both assigners
    ApproachSet.logits whatever the stage needs for its own loss
    Owns view.loss(approach, matched) -> {"loss/…": scalar, "metric/…": scalar} and its targets, computed from the labels the assigner matched
    Must have the attribute template (a ViewTemplate with as many views as the label set): the detector hands it to the assigner
    Declares label_formats (kinds of matched labels it can read: dense grids view_friction (M, V, A, D), compact scores view_graspness (M, V)), needs_matched (optional label fields it cannot do without), seed_scores (scores it adds to the seeds), applies_seed_weights (whether its loss honours the per-seed weight of a scale-balanced assigner)

    Three facts about the neighbours shape every option here:

    1. The labels know 300 directions. GraspNet-1Billion and GraspClutter6D grasp labels are indexed by a 300-view Fibonacci lattice, and the assigners address labels by view_index into it. A stage may predict on another lattice, or no lattice at all, but it must snap its choice to one of those 300 for the label lookup, and its targets can never be finer than they are.
    2. Train and inference crops differ by the snap. In training the crop frame is the label frame of the snapped view; at inference it is the frame of the direction the stage returned. For a stage whose directions are the 300 label views the two are the same up to the re-indexing of the labels under the object pose; for every other stage they differ by up to the lattice spacing (table in section 5).
    3. The head still owns the in-plane angle. All registered head bundles decode approach_to_rotation(direction, angle bin). A stage that regresses a full rotation therefore contributes its first column; the rest is an auxiliary output (RegressedApproach.frame) that is supervised and logged. A head bundle that takes the full frame from the view stage does not exist yet (section 7).

    2. Which option when¶

    You want Take Why
    the reference behaviour of GSNet / EconomicGrasp / FineGrasp template-view a score per label view, training view sampled from the scores
    the reference behaviour of GraspNet-baseline / Scale-Balanced-Grasp objectness-view, objectness-view-graspable also predicts objectness, which their decoders read
    the same view scoring, but with seed weights or as a base for the options below lattice-view computes what template-view computes (tested with shared weights), and applies the weights of ScaleBalancedAssigner
    a smaller output layer / coarser choice lattice-fibonacci-120, icosphere-162, healpix-192 fewer classes; each view is supervised by its nearest label view
    only approaches away from the camera lattice-hemisphere-60 the 60-of-120 template of RGB Matters
    a classification problem instead of 300 regressions lattice-best-view softmax cross-entropy on the most graspable view
    ... where near misses are cheap lattice-angular-cost adds the angular class loss of RGB Matters
    a direction between lattice points at inference lattice-soft-argmax softmax-weighted mean of the top-k view directions
    training crops closer to what inference will choose lattice-topk samples the training view among the 10 best views instead of all 300
    no lattice in the output at all direction-regression 3 numbers per seed, cosine loss
    ... with a confidence of the direction direction-vmf direction + concentration, von Mises-Fisher likelihood
    a full grasp frame from two vectors two-vector, two-vector-baseline Gram-Schmidt in the order of the 6-D representation, or of Contact-GraspNet
    a full rotation in a representation of your choice rotation-6d, rotation-9d, rotation-quaternion (or representation: rotvec) the mappings of RoMa

    All options that are not marked "reference" make no demand of their own: they read dense or compact labels, add no seed score, need no optional label field and apply seed weights, so the contract rules accept them behind every proposal and assigner and in front of every grouper and head bundle. With one stage replaced they are most in the words of the measured page: they work under four of the five seed-based methods. The refusal is under Scale-Balanced-Grasp, and it is the head's: its decoder multiplies by the objectness of a seed, which only ObjectnessViewStage predicts (section 4, last example).

    There are no pretrained weights for any of the new stages: all start from random initialisation. Released checkpoints of the six ported methods contain TemplateViewStage or ObjectnessViewStage; loading one into a model with another view stage leaves the view stage (and nothing else) untrained.

    What each parameterisation demands:

    Stage From the labels From the assigner From the head bundle
    lattice stages (Lattice, Icosphere, Healpix) per seed, the graspness of all 300 label views: dense view_friction (M, 300, A, D) or compact view_graspness (M, 300) encoded with the same max_friction label lookup at view_index; weight is applied when returned nothing
    DirectionViewStage the same; reduced to the single most graspable view the same nothing
    TwoVectorViewStage, RomaRotationViewStage the same, plus for the frame target the grid of the snapped view (dense friction (M, A, D)) or its labelled angle bin (compact angle (M,)) additionally crop_rotation, the label frame of the snapped view num_angle equal to the stage's (checked by the contract rules)

    4. How to use each option¶

    Every option is one --set:

    grasppanda compose --list view                       # the table of presets
grasppanda compose --show view=lattice-soft-argmax   # one preset with its notes
grasppanda compose gsnet/gsnet_sunet14_g1b-realsense_10e --set view=rotation-6d -o my.yaml
grasppanda probe my.yaml                             # 20 iterations on real frames
grasppanda train my.yaml

    Lattice stages¶

    model:
  view:
    type: LatticeViewStage        # or IcosphereViewStage / HealpixViewStage
    num_views: 300                # IcosphereViewStage: subdivisions: 2; HealpixViewStage: nside: 4
    hemisphere: false             # true: only approaches away from the camera
    target: score                 # score | best_view
    selection: argmax             # evaluation: argmax | soft_argmax
    train_selection: multinomial  # training: multinomial | topk | argmax
    top_k: null                   # used by soft_argmax and topk
    temperature: 1.0              # softmax temperature of soft_argmax
    angular_weight: 0.0           # best_view only: weight of the angular class loss
    loss: {type: SmoothL1, weight: 100.0}   # best_view: {type: CrossEntropy, weight: 1.0}
    • Size. LatticeViewStage takes any num_views; IcosphereViewStage has 10 · 4**subdivisions + 2 views (12, 42, 162, 642, 2562); HealpixViewStage has 12 · nside**2 (48, 192, 300, 768 for nside 2, 4, 5, 8). Up to 300 views every view reads its own label column; beyond 300 neighbouring views share one (section 5).
    • target: score is GSNet's: regress the min-max normalised graspness of every view. target: best_view makes it a classification of the most graspable view and logs metric/view_accuracy and metric/view_error_deg; use train_selection: argmax with it, and a CrossEntropy loss.
    • angular_weight adds loss/view_angular: the expected (1 - cos)/2 between the predicted view distribution and the best view. Set the weight of loss to 0 to train with it alone.
    • selection: soft_argmax changes inference only. Choose temperature by the range of the outputs: about 0.05 for regressed scores in [0, 1], 1.0 for logits. Keep top_k small (8) so that two separate good regions are not averaged into the bad region between them.
    • train_selection: topk restricts GSNet's training-time sampling to the top_k best views.

    Regression stages¶

    model:
  view:
    type: DirectionViewStage
    concentration: false          # true: von Mises-Fisher likelihood, logs metric/view_concentration
    direction_weight: 1.0
---
model:
  view:
    type: TwoVectorViewStage
    keep: approach                # approach (6-D order) | baseline (Contact-GraspNet order)
    direction_weight: 1.0         # loss/view: 1 - cos to the most graspable label view
    frame_weight: 1.0             # loss/view_frame: squared Frobenius distance, half-turn aware
    num_angle: 12
    • loss/view pulls the direction to the most graspable label view; metric/view_error_deg is the mean angle to it.
    • loss/view_frame (two-vector and RoMa stages) compares the regressed frame with the label frame of the snapped view at its best in-plane angle, taking the smaller of the distances to that frame and to its half-turn copy (a parallel-jaw grasp turned by 180° about its approach is the same grasp). metric/view_frame_error_deg is the mean rotation angle.
    • representation of RomaRotationViewStage: 6d and 9d are continuous parameterisations; quaternion and rotvec are not and are there for comparison. rotvec is unit-tested and has no preset and no probe record.
    • residual: true (all new stages) adds the hidden features of the stage to the seed features that the grouper and the head consume, as GSNet's stage does.

    5. Pitfalls met while building this¶

    • A finer lattice does not give finer labels. Measured with 200 000 random directions (grasppanda.ops lattices, this machine):
    Lattice Views Random direction to nearest view, mean / max View to its nearest label view, mean / max Label columns read
    Fibonacci (the labels) 300 4.5° / 9.0° 0° 300
    Fibonacci 120 7.1° / 14.1° 4.6° / 7.7° 120
    Fibonacci 1200 2.2° / 4.4° 4.5° / 8.2° 300
    icosphere 42 11.9° / 20.8° 4.8° / 7.7° 42
    icosphere 162 6.1° / 10.8° 4.5° / 7.7° 162
    icosphere 642 3.0° / 5.4° 4.5° / 8.1° 300
    HEALPix 48 11.2° / 23.4° 4.5° / 6.7° 48
    HEALPix 192 5.6° / 11.5° 4.5° / 7.9° 190
    HEALPix 300 4.5° / 9.2° 4.4° / 8.3° 274
    HEALPix 768 2.8° / 5.6° 4.5° / 8.4° 300

    The third column is the price of a coarse lattice; the fourth is the train/inference crop mismatch of any lattice that is not the label lattice. HEALPix with 192 views already maps two pairs of views onto the same label column. - TemplateViewStage(num_views=N) with N ≠ 300 is not "another lattice size". Its template is also the label lattice, so the assigner refuses the labels at the first loss. Use LatticeViewStage(num_views=N). - Soft-arg-max needs a temperature that fits the outputs. With regressed scores and temperature: 1.0 the weights are nearly uniform and the mean direction is meaningless; with all views voting (top_k: null) opposite views can cancel (the stage then falls back to the best view). - A single best view is a brittle target. best_view, direction-regression and the frame stages reduce the 300 scores of a seed to their arg-max; on a symmetric object two distant views can tie and the target flips between them. target: score and angular_weight do not have this problem to the same degree. - Flip augmentation yields mirrored label frames. crop_rotation of a mirrored object pose is a reflection; the frame loss skips those seeds (their direction loss still counts). - The vMF loss is a log-density and goes negative when the predicted concentration is high. That is not a bug; do not compare its value with the cosine loss. - RomaRotationViewStage imports RoMa in every forward (through the cached require), not as a stored module attribute: a module attribute would make the detector impossible to deep-copy. - healpy is GPL-2.0, so the bridge uses the BSD-licensed healpix package, which ships wheels and needs only NumPy.

    6. Not wrapped¶

    Candidate Opened Why not
    Contact-GraspNet code (two-vector head, contact labels, 5-point loss) NVlabs/contact_graspnet its License.pdf is the "NVIDIA Source Code License for Contact-GraspNet"; section 3.3 limits use to non-commercial research and evaluation. The idea of its parameterisation is two-vector-baseline; its contact-point labels and control-point loss need a label asset the toolbox does not have
    vMF-Contact code YitianShi/vMF-Contact no licence file (the licence request returns 404). The closed form of the likelihood is textbook and is written here; its evidential posterior update is not
    Generalizing-Grasp code (regressed view with a neighbourhood term, 2θ angle regression) mahaoxiang822/Generalizing-Grasp no licence file. direction-regression is the cosine part of the idea; its soft neighbourhood term is not included
    S4G (6-D rotation per point, symmetric loss) yzqin/s4g-release no licence file. The same representation and a half-turn-aware loss are in rotation-6d / two-vector
    DexGraspNet 2.0 (diffusion on seed features) PKU-EPIC/DexGraspNet2 no licence file; a generative head is a head bundle, not a view stage
    OrbitGrasp, Edge Grasp Network BoceHu/orbitgrasp (MIT), HaojHuang/Edge-Grasp-Network (MIT) detectors of their own (equivariant networks, edge features) with their own simulated labels; they do not fit a seed-feature view stage. Only the licences were checked here
    healpy, astropy-healpix healpy (GPL-2.0), astropy-healpix (BSD-3-Clause) GPL; and a dependency on astropy for 12·nside² vectors. healpix does the same with NumPy only
    PyTorch3D rotation conversions facebookresearch/pytorch3d a compiled package for functions RoMa has in pure PyTorch
    Top-k views per seed at inference (several grasps per location) - ApproachSet is one row per seed and the heads return one grasp per seed; it needs a change of the skeleton, not a view stage
    A head bundle that takes the regressed in-plane rotation - belongs to the head slot; until it exists the frame of the rotation stages is auxiliary
